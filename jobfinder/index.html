How to use JobFinder

  1. Type a job title and press Enter. Add as many as you like. Paste a comma-separated list to add several at once.
  2. Add your city and state. Optional, but without both the Location links stay greyed out. Remote links work either way.
  3. Click a link to search that board. Every title gets a card, every board gets a row. Location searches near you, Remote searches remote-only. Each opens in a new tab.
  4. Watch the receipts. Each click gets stamped with the time, like ✓ 9:15 AM. Tomorrow it says 1d ago, and after three days it turns amber, 3d ago, so you know what you've been skipping. The card header counts how many boards you've hit today.
  5. Flip the grouping with the button above the cards to see one card per board instead of one per title. Handy for "let me clear out LinkedIn."
  6. Edit anytime. Click a title to rename it, and its history comes along. Clear the text or hit × to remove it.

Everything lives in this browser only. No account, no sync, nothing leaves your machine. Clearing your browser's site data wipes the slate.
